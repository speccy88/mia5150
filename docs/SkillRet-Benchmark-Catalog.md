# SkillRet benchmark catalog

This catalog covers **17 studies and bounded pilots**, including the new matched proposal study below. The historical table covers **16 completed studies and bounded pilots** in this repository. Rows describe recorded execution; a study can contain completed panels alongside failed, partial, superseded, or unrun arms. They are not 16 independent, fully completed evaluations of the entire dataset.

Historical table reviewed **10 October 2026** against [EXPERIMENTS.md][index] and the matching study documentation/evidence at the [reviewed source snapshot][commit]. No inference, training, paid request, or benchmark rerun was performed to prepare that historical table. The added row 17 records new local inference on 24 October.

**Environment labels:** Local = documented local/on-machine execution; Cloud = documented remote compute or hosted inference; Hybrid = local preparation/retrieval/evaluation combined with cloud inference. Hardware and provider details are included only where recorded. The CPU archive documents its processor and runtime but not its physical hosting, so that row explicitly leaves Local/Cloud unresolved.

Unless stated otherwise, SkillRet refers to `ThakiCloud/SKILLRET` revision `6583d7d2ed07644d0fb8938ed8178f3a7dc42a12`, with **4,392 test queries, 6,006 test skills, and 7,187 positive qrels**. A full catalog, a full query count, or a separately frozen pilot does not make these previously exposed public evaluations blind. Different document views, context limits, candidate pools, and denominators are not directly comparable.

## Reading the metrics and execution status

- **Recall@k** usually averages the fraction of every query's positive qrels retrieved. Rows 1–2 use generated single-target, name-based scoring; their match/hit rates have a different meaning. **Hit@1** and Recall@1 are not interchangeable when a query has several positives.
- **Complete/Completeness@k** means all labeled positives were retrieved. It differs from **exact-set match**, which also rejects extra selected IDs. **MRR** retains the cutoff used by the source: row 4's saved reciprocal rank must not be relabeled MRR@5, row 5 uses MRR@15, and other rows explicitly use MRR@5/10.
- **Candidate recall, positive-pair coverage and pool-oracle scores** describe the available pool or its ceiling. They do not establish downstream reranker accuracy or skill-execution success.
- **Latency** keeps its measured boundary: warm inference/search, API batch time, startup, or a component-sum estimate. Cache reuse is not zero-cost fresh serving. Unmeasured deployment end-to-end latency is not inferred from these components.
- **Costs** keep their evidence type: provider-returned costs, metered usage, estimates, observed credit differences, and held reservations are distinct. Overlapping/reused smoke and pilot calls are counted once. Missing usage and uncertain charges remain unknown.
- **Completed** applies only to the stated panel. Invalid outputs, context errors, resource stops, interrupted checkpoints and unattempted cases remain visible. Code support, offline fixture tests, or a planned expansion do not count as a completed experiment.
